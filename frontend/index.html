Scientific Findings & Affect Insights

  • Cross-Modal Disambiguation: Disagreement between literal verbal transcripts and facial tone is resolved dynamically by the attention network rather than hardcoded heuristics.
  • Missing-Modality Masking: When video is unavailable, the network automatically shifts attention to linguistic and acoustic features without performance collapse.
  • Acoustic-Prosodic Anchoring: Acoustic energy differentiates high-arousal emotions (Anger, Surprise) from low-arousal states (Sadness, Neutral).
  • Visual Dominance for Subtle Cues: Facial micro-expressions provide the highest discriminative power for masked or sarcastic dialogue utterances.
